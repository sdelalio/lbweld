Capabilities

What we do

Pulsed and CW laser welding

Nd:YAG and fiber lasers, keyhole or conduction mode, matched to your joint. Spot, seam, stitch and rotary welds.

Weld development

Weld schedule development and qualification, first articles and requalification limits defined before production.

Custom fixturing

Fixtures built for fit up and repeatability, with microscope and camera joint alignment on multi axis CNC stations.

Hard to join materials

Reflective, dissimilar and exotic metals. Filler wire or shims when the metallurgy is not compatible.

Metallographic lab

Cut, polish and etch cross sections in house to measure penetration, weld width, fusion profile and heat affected zone.

Partner network

Electron beam welding and work requiring ISO 13485, Nadcap or AS9100 through vetted partners we know well.
