About us

Precision welding has been our family business since 1965.

Weld Solutions is small on purpose. We are flexible, nimble and easy to work with, and every weld is backed by decades of precision welding experience.

Where we come from

The story starts with George DeLalio, who founded EB Industries in Farmingdale, NY in the mid 1960s. With an $8,000 loan from his mother, he bought a single electron beam welding machine and built one of the most respected precision welding job shops in the country, serving the growing aerospace industry.

In 1984, Steven DeLalio brought laser welding into EB Industries when the company commissioned its first Raytheon pulsed laser welder. He has followed the technology ever since, adding real time weld depth measurement from Laser Depth Dynamics (LDD), adjustable beam mode (ABM) fiber lasers and a range of wobble heads as each came along. Laser welding has advanced at a rapid pace since that first machine, and we have advanced with it.

In 1993, Steven became president and owner. Over the next 26 years he grew revenue eightfold and added hermetic sealing, before EB Industries was acquired by MCM Capital in 2019.

Steven never lost his passion for the industry. He still consults on welding and laser technology, and he recently began offering welding services after seeing a real need for quick delivery and high quality at a price that does not carry the cost of tremendous overhead. That is what Weld Solutions is built to do.

Leadership and team

Steven DeLalio

President
