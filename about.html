About us

Precision welding has been our family business since 1965.

Weld Solutions is small on purpose. Flexible, nimble and easy to work with, with the experience of a much larger shop behind every weld.

Where we come from

The story starts with George DeLalio, who founded EB Industries in Farmingdale, NY in the mid 1960s. With an $8,000 loan from his mother, he bought a single electron beam welding machine and built one of the most respected precision welding job shops in the country, serving the growing aerospace industry.

In 1993, his son Steven DeLalio became president and owner. Over more than 25 years, Steven grew revenue eightfold and added laser welding and hermetic sealing, before EB Industries was acquired by MCM Capital.

Weld Solutions carries that experience forward as a focused laser welding job shop, with the same standards and a lot less overhead between you and the people doing the work.
