Medical and Surgical

Bone screws and orthopedic components

Small, high value titanium and stainless parts where penetration, cosmetics and traceability all matter.

Typical materialsTi-6Al-4V, 316L, 17-4 PH

Modular surgical drill bits

Fit pin welds into the bit body, welded from both sides to a controlled weld zone, then electropolished.

Typical materials17-4 PH, 455, 465 stainless

Surgical instrument assemblies

Pins, nuts and latches welded into handle and broach assemblies, autogenous, to AWS D17.1 when specified.

Typical materials300 and 400 series stainless

Implantable device housings

Seam welds on titanium enclosures where the weld must be sound, clean and leak tight.

Typical materialsCP titanium, Ti-6Al-4V, MP35N

Aerospace, Electronics and Sensors

Precision sensors and transducers

Diaphragm and cap welds on pressure and temperature sensors with tight heat input control.

Typical materialsInconel, 17-4 PH, Kovar

Waveguides

Flange and seam welds on waveguide and RF components where distortion has to stay near zero.

Typical materialsAluminum, copper, brass

Hermetic packages and connectors

Lid seal welds on electronic packages and feedthroughs, built on decades of hermetic sealing work.

Typical materialsKovar, aluminum, stainless

Energy and Industrial

Battery and alternative energy assemblies

Tab and bus bar welds on cells and packs, including reflective and dissimilar metals.

Typical materialsNickel, copper, aluminum

Shafts, pins and tubes

Rotary all around welds on shafts, pins and tubes, from one prototype to production lots.

Typical materialsStainless, alloy steels, Inconel

Filament and emitter assemblies

Fine wire filament assemblies for electron beam and X-ray sources, welded on fixtures we build.

Typical materialsTungsten, tantalum, molybdenum

Materials

Including but not limited to 300 and 400 series stainless, 17-4 PH, 15-5 PH, 455 and 465. Cold rolled, carbon and alloy steels. Aluminum 1100, 2219, 4047, 5052 and 6061. Copper, beryllium copper and brass. Titanium and Ti-6Al-4V. Inconel, Hastelloy and Monel. Kovar and Invar. MP35N and cobalt alloys. Nitinol. Tantalum, niobium and molybdenum. Platinum, gold, silver and palladium.

Joint types

  • Butt, lap, fillet and edge welds
  • Spot, seam and stitch welds
  • Rotary all around welds on shafts, pins and tubes
  • Hermetic lid and seal welds
  • Autogenous, or with filler wire or shims
